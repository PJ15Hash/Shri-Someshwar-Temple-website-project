Festivals & Celebrations

The temple celebrates several major festivals every year with great grandeur and community participation:

  • Maha Shivratri — Night-long vigil with abhishekam, rudrabhisheka, and bhajans
  • Chaitra Purnima — Annual community feast and special procession
  • Ganesh Chaturthi — Ganesha pooja and cultural programmes
  • Makar Sankranti — Tilgul distribution and special poojas
  • Naga Panchami — Worship of the Naga shrine with milk abhisheka
  • Ugadi — Kannada & Tulu New Year celebrations
